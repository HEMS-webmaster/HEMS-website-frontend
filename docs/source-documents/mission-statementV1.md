# Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide a platform for scientists, engineers, and users to come together, exchange knowledge, and address common challenges related to deploying mass spectrometry technology outside a controlled laboratory environment. By facilitating interaction and cooperation, we aim to accelerate the realization of reliable, compact, and energy-efficient mass spectrometry solutions for demanding, and at times extremely harsh, environments.

## Objectives

- **Facilitate Collaboration** — We strive to create an inclusive community that encourages cross-disciplinary collaboration among scientists and engineers working in the field of mass spectrometry.
- **Promote Enabling Technologies** — Engage with groups that focus on enabling technologies that enhance the capabilities of portable mass spectrometers. These include advancements in power management, sample introduction, signal processing, and miniaturization techniques to improve performance and expand application possibilities.
- **Knowledge Exchange** — Through the HEMS Workshop, focus groups at conferences, and publications, we promote the dissemination of cutting-edge research, innovative technologies, and practical experiences related to mass spectrometry deployment in challenging environments.
- **Foster Innovation** — The HEMS Society is committed to fostering innovation and development efforts that enhance the reliability, portability, efficiency, and performance of mass spectrometers in various harsh environments.
- **Outreach and Education** — We engage with the public, researchers, and industry stakeholders to raise awareness about the importance and potential of portable mass spectrometry in field applications.
