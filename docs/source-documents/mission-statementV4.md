# Mission Statement

Our mission is to bring together scientists, engineers, and field operators to exchange knowledge and solve the shared challenges of deploying mass spectrometry outside the laboratory. Through open, cross-disciplinary cooperation, we accelerate the realization of reliable, compact, and energy-efficient instruments for demanding, and at times extremely harsh, environments—for example, the deep ocean, upper atmosphere, planetary surfaces, industrial sites, and defense and security operations.

## Objectives

- **Build an Inclusive Community** — We bring together instrument developers, analytical scientists, and end-users from academia, government laboratories, and industry in an open, welcoming forum for collaboration.
- **Advance Enabling Technologies** — We champion progress in the subsystems that make field mass spectrometry possible, including miniaturization, compact vacuum systems, sample introduction, power management, and onboard signal processing.
- **Share Practical Knowledge** — Through the HEMS Workshop, conference focus groups, and our open-access proceedings, we share cutting-edge research alongside candid lessons from real-world deployments.
- **Bridge the Lab and the Field** — We help move promising concepts from the laboratory into operational use, improving the ruggedness, autonomy, and analytical performance of in-situ instruments.
- **Educate and Mentor** — Through Student Travel Awards, mentorship, and public outreach, we support the next generation of field mass spectrometrists and raise awareness of what in-situ chemical analysis can do.
