# Mission Statement

Our mission is to convene scientists, engineers, and field operators to exchange knowledge and solve the shared challenges of deploying mass spectrometry outside the controlled laboratory. Through open, cross-sector cooperation, we accelerate reliable, compact, and energy-efficient mass spectrometry for demanding—and at times extreme—environments across ocean, atmospheric, planetary, industrial, and security frontiers.

## Objectives

- **Facilitate Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration** — We unite instrument builders, analytical scientists, and end-users across academia, government laboratories, and industry in an open, single-track community.
- **Advance Enabling Technologies** — We champion breakthroughs in subsystem engineering—including compact vacuum systems, harsh-environment sample inlets, ruggedized analyzers, power management, and onboard signal processing—that expand what field mass spectrometers can measure.
- **Share Candid Field Knowledge** — Through the biennial HEMS Workshop, conference focus groups, and our open-access proceedings archive, we disseminate cutting-edge research, practical deployment lessons, and candid engineering insights from the field.
- **Accelerate Field-Ready Innovation** — We bridge the gap between laboratory concepts and operational deployment, advancing the ruggedness, autonomy, analytical performance, and environmental survivability of in-situ instrumentation.
- **Cultivate Education & Emerging Investigators** — Through competitive Student Travel Awards, mentorship, and community outreach, we support the next generation of field mass spectrometrists and broaden awareness of in-situ chemical sensing.

## V1 → V2 Changelog & Editorial Rationale

**Baseline Reference:** [`mission-statementV1.md`](./mission-statementV1.md)  
**Prepared By:** `@prod` & `@brand` (2026-11-12)

### 1. Cross-Cutting Structural & Stylistic Improvements

1. **Grammatical Parallelism Across Objective Titles:**
   - **V1:** Mixed three *Verb + Noun* headings (*Facilitate Collaboration*, *Promote Enabling Technologies*, *Foster Innovation*) with two bare *Noun* headings (*Knowledge Exchange*, *Outreach and Education*).
   - **V2:** Standardized all five titles as parallel **Imperative Verb + Domain Noun Phrase** commitments (*Facilitate…*, *Advance…*, *Share…*, *Accelerate…*, *Cultivate…*).
   - **Reasoning:** Parallel charter headings read with stronger institutional authority and scannability on both web layouts and 501(c)(3) grant/sponsorship dossiers.

2. **Unified Active First-Person Plural Voice (`"We ..."`):**
   - **V1:** Shifted voice across bullets—Bullet 1 used `"We strive..."`, Bullet 2 dropped its subject into a sentence fragment (`"Engage with groups..."`), Bullet 3 used `"Through..., we promote..."`, and Bullet 4 switched to third-person (`"The HEMS Society is committed to fostering..."`) while repeating its own heading verb.
   - **V2:** Unified all five objectives in a direct, active first-person plural voice (`"We unite..."`, `"We champion..."`, `"...we disseminate..."`, `"We bridge..."`, `"...we support..."`).
   - **Reasoning:** Eliminates the grammatical fragment in Objective 2, removes self-referential repetition in Objective 4, and gives the charter a cohesive, confident voice.

3. **Full Harsh-Environment Scope Instead of Narrow `"Portable"` Framing:**
   - **V1:** Referred twice to *"portable mass spectrometers"* (Objectives 2 and 5).
   - **V2:** Expanded terminology to **field, in-situ, and autonomous instrumentation** across ocean, atmospheric, planetary, industrial, and security regimes.
   - **Reasoning:** Many core HEMS platforms—deep-sea benthic landers and ROVs/AUVs, Mars/Titan spacecraft payloads, stratospheric aircraft, and fixed harsh-process monitors—are not hand-carried *"portable"* units. Broader *in-situ / field* language accurately represents the entire HEMS community.

### 2. Section-by-Section Change Log

#### Mission Statement
- **V1:** *"Our mission is to provide a platform for scientists, engineers, and users to come together, exchange knowledge, and address common challenges related to deploying mass spectrometry technology outside a controlled laboratory environment. By facilitating interaction and cooperation, we aim to accelerate the realization of reliable, compact, and energy-efficient mass spectrometry solutions for demanding, and at times extremely harsh, environments."*
- **V2:** *"Our mission is to convene scientists, engineers, and field operators to exchange knowledge and solve the shared challenges of deploying mass spectrometry outside the controlled laboratory. Through open, cross-sector cooperation, we accelerate reliable, compact, and energy-efficient mass spectrometry for demanding—and at times extreme—environments across ocean, atmospheric, planetary, industrial, and security frontiers."*
- **Changes & Reasoning:**
  1. Replaced passive/indirect phrasing (*"provide a platform for... to come together"*, *"aim to accelerate the realization of... solutions"*) with direct verbs (**"convene"**, **"solve"**, **"accelerate"**).
  2. Refined *"users"* to **"field operators"** to highlight hands-on mission practitioners alongside scientists and engineers.
  3. Added **"open, cross-sector cooperation"** and explicitly anchored the five signature HEMS deployment domains (**ocean, atmospheric, planetary, industrial, and security frontiers**) so first-time visitors and sponsors immediately grasp where harsh-environment mass spectrometry operates.

#### Objective 01: *Facilitate Collaboration* → *Facilitate Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration*
- **V1:** *"We strive to create an inclusive community that encourages cross-disciplinary collaboration among scientists and engineers working in the field of mass spectrometry."*
- **V2:** *"We unite instrument builders, analytical scientists, and end-users across academia, government laboratories, and industry in an open, single-track community."*
- **Changes & Reasoning:**
  1. Restored **end-users** (which V1 included in the Mission Statement but dropped in Objective 1).
  2. Named the three institutional pillars of HEMS (**academia, government laboratories, and industry**) and the Workshop's signature **open, single-track** structure rather than generic *"strive to create"* phrasing.

#### Objective 02: *Promote Enabling Technologies* → *Advance Enabling Technologies*
- **V1:** *"Engage with groups that focus on enabling technologies that enhance the capabilities of portable mass spectrometers. These include advancements in power management, sample introduction, signal processing, and miniaturization techniques to improve performance and expand application possibilities."*
- **V2:** *"We champion breakthroughs in subsystem engineering—including compact vacuum systems, harsh-environment sample inlets, ruggedized analyzers, power management, and onboard signal processing—that expand what field mass spectrometers can measure."*
- **Changes & Reasoning:**
  1. Fixed the missing-subject sentence fragment (*"Engage with groups..."* → *"We champion..."*).
  2. Added **compact vacuum systems** and **ruggedized analyzers** alongside sample inlets, power management, and onboard signal processing—capturing the defining hardware bottlenecks of harsh-environment mass spectrometry.
  3. Replaced *"portable mass spectrometers"* with *"field mass spectrometers"*.

#### Objective 03: *Knowledge Exchange* → *Share Candid Field Knowledge*
- **V1:** *"Through the HEMS Workshop, focus groups at conferences, and publications, we promote the dissemination of cutting-edge research, innovative technologies, and practical experiences related to mass spectrometry deployment in challenging environments."*
- **V2:** *"Through the biennial HEMS Workshop, conference focus groups, and our open-access proceedings archive, we disseminate cutting-edge research, practical deployment lessons, and candid engineering insights from the field."*
- **Changes & Reasoning:**
  1. Converted bare noun heading (*Knowledge Exchange*) into parallel verb-led form (**Share Candid Field Knowledge**).
  2. Explicitly named the **open-access proceedings archive** (a major public asset of the 501(c)(3) Society, spanning 1999–present).
  3. Highlighted **practical deployment lessons and candid engineering insights**—capturing HEMS's unique symposium culture where investigators openly discuss real-world field failures and engineering trade-offs, not just polished commercial results.

#### Objective 04: *Foster Innovation* → *Accelerate Field-Ready Innovation*
- **V1:** *"The HEMS Society is committed to fostering innovation and development efforts that enhance the reliability, portability, efficiency, and performance of mass spectrometers in various harsh environments."*
- **V2:** *"We bridge the gap between laboratory concepts and operational deployment, advancing the ruggedness, autonomy, analytical performance, and environmental survivability of in-situ instrumentation."*
- **Changes & Reasoning:**
  1. Eliminated near-duplicate overlap with Sentence 2 of the Mission Statement (*"reliable, compact, and energy-efficient... harsh environments"*).
  2. Defined a distinct strategic role for Objective 4: **translating benchtop/laboratory prototypes into operationally survivable, autonomous in-situ instruments** (TRL maturation and environmental hardening).

#### Objective 05: *Outreach and Education* → *Cultivate Education & Emerging Investigators*
- **V1:** *"We engage with the public, researchers, and industry stakeholders to raise awareness about the importance and potential of portable mass spectrometry in field applications."*
- **V2:** *"Through competitive Student Travel Awards, mentorship, and community outreach, we support the next generation of field mass spectrometrists and broaden awareness of in-situ chemical sensing."*
- **Changes & Reasoning:**
  1. Converted bare noun heading (*Outreach and Education*) into parallel verb-led form (**Cultivate Education & Emerging Investigators**).
  2. Explicitly anchored the Society's flagship educational charity program—**competitive Student Travel Awards and early-career mentorship**—while retaining broader public/stakeholder outreach and replacing *"portable mass spectrometry"* with *"in-situ chemical sensing"*.
