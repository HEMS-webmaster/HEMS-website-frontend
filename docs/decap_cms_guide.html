01 / Introduction

Welcome to the HEMS Content Manager

The HEMS Content Manager (powered by Decap CMS) allows authorized society organizers and committee chairs to update website text, announce deadlines, and adjust hotel accommodations directly from any desktop web browser — without writing a single line of code.

🛡️ 100% Safe & Sandboxed
You cannot break the website, server code, or research proceedings. Master archives are hard-locked.
👁️ Real-Time Visual Preview
See your formatted changes in real time side-by-side as you type before saving.
⚡ Automatic Publishing
When you click Publish, the website automatically rebuilds and deploys in under two minutes.
02 / Getting Started

How to Access & Log In

The Content Manager lives right inside the HEMS website at the dedicated administrative address:

https://hems-workshop.org/admin/ (Or http://localhost:3000/admin/ on local staging)

Logging In:

  1. Navigate to /admin/ in any standard web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
  2. Click the blue "Login with GitHub" button.
  3. Authorize with your authorized GitHub user account.
  4. You will immediately enter the HEMS Content Manager dashboard.

04 / Content Collections

The 8 Managed Pages & Announcements

You have full editing access to the following 8 areas of the website. Each page provides dedicated form fields tailored to its specific purpose:

1. About the Society (/about)

Update society purpose, 501(c)(3) tax status statement, workshop history summary, and upcoming workshop edition details.

title, subtitle, body

2. Call for Papers (/call-for-papers)

Set abstract submission deadlines, toggle the "Accepting Submissions" badge on/off, paste submission portal links, and update focus topic lists.

has toggle switch

3. Workshop Accommodations (/accommodations)

Change the conference hotel name, street address, group discount reservation code (e.g. "HEMS2025"), room rate, cutoff date, and airport transit directions.

hotel, code, rate, dates

4. Workshop Registration (/registration)

Manage registration fee tiers (Regular, Student, Exhibitor), early-bird deadlines, payment portals, and refund/cancellation policies.

fee schedules

5. Abstract Formatting Guidelines (/formatting-guidelines)

Provide extended abstract structural specifications, font limits, page limits, and download links for the Word template.

template links

6. Corporate Sponsorship Opportunities (/corporate-sponsorship)

Update sponsorship package pricing (Platinum, Gold, Silver), booth exhibitor benefits, and sponsor prospectus PDF links.

tier pricing

7. Contact Us (/contact)

Update society contact email addresses (General, Technical Program, Treasurer) and physical mailing addresses.

emails, address

8. Announcements & News (/announcements)

Publish new announcements, news posts, or urgent banner alerts (e.g. "Submission deadline extended to July 15!").

create new posts
05 / Step-by-Step Guide

How to Edit & Publish (4 Simple Steps)

Step 1: Select Your Page

Click on "Website Marketing Pages" in the left sidebar, then click on the title of the page you wish to edit (for example, "Workshop Accommodations").

Step 2: Update Your Information

The editor displays simple input boxes for each piece of information. Simply type your changes into the appropriate box (e.g. change the Group Discount Code to HEMS2026 or adjust the Room Rate).

Step 3: Review the Visual Preview

On the right half of your screen, a live preview pane shows exactly how your formatted text, bold styling, and headings will appear on the website.

Step 4: Save & Publish

Click the green "Save" button at the top. When you are ready for your changes to go live on the public internet, select "Publish". The cloud system will automatically build the site and deploy your updates within ~2 minutes!

06 / Safety Guarantee

The Safety Perimeter (What is Protected)

Clients often worry: "What if I make a mistake and delete a research paper or break the site?"

🔒 Built-in Architectural Protection

The HEMS system has a hard-coded security perimeter. Decap CMS is physically restricted so that it can never access or overwrite:

  • 25 Years of Proceedings: All 1,590 scientific slide decks, abstracts, and programs are isolated in cloud storage.
  • Executive Governance: Board member rosters and reviewer scores are protected behind administrator authentication.
  • Software Code & Server Configs: Next.js code, Firebase rules, and build scripts are completely invisible to the editor.

08 / Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for published changes to appear online?

Once you click "Publish", the website automatically re-compiles and deploys via Firebase Hosting. Changes are usually live globally within 60 to 120 seconds.

Can I undo a change if I make a typo?

Yes! You can either re-edit the text and click Publish again, or ask the HEMS webmaster to restore any previous version, as all changes are saved with full version history in GitHub.

Who do I contact if I need access or help?

Contact the HEMS technical administrator at webmaster@hems-workshop.org to invite a new user account or report any questions.
