Field-Portable, Fast GC/TOFMS Authors: Jack Syage, Brian Nies, Rick Harkewicz Institute: Syagen Technology, Inc. ABSTRACT This talk reports on a fast gas chromatograph, time-of-flight mass spectrometer (GC/TOFMS) for man-portable field use. The complete system is designed to meet performance, size, weight, power, cost, and ruggedness requirements for a laboratory in the field. The core technology is also adaptable to specific applications including real-time point detection for hazardous chemical releases (e.g., chemical weapons), for biological agent signature identification, and for mobile monitoring platforms (e.g., air, ship, truck).
