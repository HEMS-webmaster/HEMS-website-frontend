Olson Institute: Idaho National Engineering and Environmental Laboratory (INEEL) ABSTRACT In the event that chemical weapons are released somewhere in an urban area of the United States how will it be determined that the area is decontaminated to the point it can be opened to the public? Chemical weapons in an urban environment present unique challenges to decontamination and characterization because once released they are partitioned between different physical and chemical states, which dictate their mobility, toxicity, and susceptibility to treatment.
