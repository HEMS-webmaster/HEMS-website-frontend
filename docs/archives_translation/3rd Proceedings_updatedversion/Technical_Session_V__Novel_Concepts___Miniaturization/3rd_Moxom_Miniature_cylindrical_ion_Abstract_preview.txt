Miniature cylindrical ion trap mass spectrometry Authors: Jeremy Moxom, William Whitten, Peter Reilly, Michael Ramsey Institute: Oak Ridge National Laboratory ABSTRACT The development of miniature ion trap mass spectrometers is being driven by many new applications, such as, autonomous underwater vehicles, planetary probes, monitoring air quality in spacecraft and field portable mass spectrometers for the detection of BW/CW agents. Miniaturization of the trap electrodes to sub-millimeter sizes significantly reduces the working voltages and pumping requirements, resulting in a substantial reduction in size and power consumption compared to a conventional instrument, without a significant degradation in overall performance.
