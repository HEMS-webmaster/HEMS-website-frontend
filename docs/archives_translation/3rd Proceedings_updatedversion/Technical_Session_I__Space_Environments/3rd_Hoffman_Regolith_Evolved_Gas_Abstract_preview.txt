Regolith Evolved Gas Analyzer (REGA): An Instrument to Characterize the Martial Soil Mineralogy and Atmospheric Composition Authors: John H. Hoffman Institute: University of Texas at Dallas ABSTRACT The Regolith Evolved Gas Analyzer (REGA), a high temperature furnace, soil handling/metering system and mass spectrometer instrument, for measuring and determining the reactivity and mineralogical composition of soil samples was develop for a future Mars lander mission. REGA would provide key mineralogical and reactivity data that is needed to understand the soil chemistry, to identify locations of possible ancient life on Mars, and to determine potential hazards to human explorers and equipment.
