Underwater Mass Spectrometers for Detection of VOCs and Dissolved Gases Authors: Gottfried Kibelka, Tim Short, David Fries Institute: Center for Ocean Technology, University of South Florida ABSTRACT We have developed and deployed mass spectrometry systems that are capable of autonomous underwater operation for chemical analysis. Our primary focus is to analyze coastal marine waters for natural and anthropogenic organic compounds and gases. The high spatial and temporal resolutions of the underwater mass spectrometer (MS) systems has been used to monitor and map chemical distributions in harsh environments.
