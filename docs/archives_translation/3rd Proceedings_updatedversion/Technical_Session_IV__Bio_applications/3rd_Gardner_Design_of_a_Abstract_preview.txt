Design of a Novel Miniature MALDI-TOF Mass Spectrometer for High Throughput Medical Screening Authors: Ben Gardner, Robert English, Robert Cotter Institute: Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine ABSTRACT Our laboratory has been developing a series of mid-resolution miniature MALDI-TOF mass spectrometers for the detection of biomarker signatures. Applications benefiting from these devices include high throughput biological screening assays and real-time biological weapons detection. As we continue to decrease the overall size of our designs, several fundamental parameters become increasingly important to achieving suitable resolving power.
