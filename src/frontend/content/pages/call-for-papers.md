The Harsh-Environment Mass Spectrometry (HEMS) Workshop offers a unique forum for scientists, engineers, and researchers to discuss challenges and innovations in in situ mass spectrometry. Technical submissions detail novel research, instrumentation advances, field deployment results, and experimental methodologies.

### Presentation Formats
* **Oral Presentations:** Technical talks scheduled throughout plenary and technical sessions. Requires single-page PDF abstract submission.
* **Poster Presentations:** Interactive session for direct discussion and detailed hardware schematics. Highly recommended for graduate students.

### Core Research Areas
* **Field Deployments:** Deep-sea vent exploration, planetary probes, battlefield scenarios, and environmental monitoring in isolated zones.
* **Miniaturization:** Low-power analyzer components, portable vacuum systems, micro-machined ion sources, and ruggedized electronics.
* **Data Processing:** Onboard spectral matching libraries, compression algorithms, low-bandwidth telemetry, and autonomous sampling routines.
