The HEMS Workshop provides an exceptional opportunity to connect directly with decision-makers, lead researchers, and instrumentation specialists from NASA, NOAA, DoD, academic institutions, and leading commercial instrumentation manufacturers.

### Sponsorship Levels
* **Platinum Sponsor ($5,000):** Prominent logo placement on website hero and proceedings cover, 2 complimentary full registrations, 1 exhibition booth table, and formal verbal acknowledgment at the conference banquet.
* **Gold Sponsor ($3,000):** Logo on website and proceedings, 1 complimentary registration, 1 exhibition booth table, and acknowledgement during plenary sessions.
* **Silver Sponsor ($1,500):** Logo on website and proceedings catalog, and promotional literature distribution in attendee welcome bags.
