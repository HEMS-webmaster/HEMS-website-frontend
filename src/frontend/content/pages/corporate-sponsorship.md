The HEMS Workshop is an all-volunteer 501(c)(3) organization that provides an open forum to bridge the gap between academic, industrial, and government circles. With no formal membership or membership fee, each biennial workshop is independently funded through donations, registration fees, and corporate sponsors.

Your registration as a corporate sponsor directly offsets the operational costs of hosting the workshop and is instrumental in helping us provide competitive Student Travel Awards.

### Sponsor Benefits
* **Hardware & Literature Exhibition:** Space at the workshop to display your instruments, hardware, and technical literature.
* **Corporate Technical Poster:** Invitation to display a corporate poster alongside technical sessions (or have our committee post it on your behalf if non-attending).
* **Workshop Recognition:** Prominent acknowledgment on the HEMS Workshop banner and in symposium program materials.
* **Website Logo & Link:** Year-round placement on the HEMS website featuring your company logo and a link back to your website.

### Participation & Flexible Invoicing Options
* **Standard Full Package ($1,350 USD):** Includes full corporate sponsorship benefits plus one (1) full attendee registration.
* **Split-Payment Option ($850 + $500 USD):** Separates the $850 corporate sponsorship fee from the $500 attendee registration fee via `shop.hems-workshop.org` to accommodate internal corporate spending limits.
* **Sponsor Only / Non-Attending ($850 USD):** Remote workshop support with arranged corporate poster display alongside full banner, program, and website recognition.
