Your registration as a corporate sponsor directly offsets the costs of organizing the biennial workshop and plays a vital role in funding student travel awards.

### Sponsorship Packages
* **Standard Package ($1,350 USD):** Includes full corporate sponsorship benefits, exhibition display table, banner/website recognition, plus one (1) full attendee registration.
* **Split-Payment Option ($850 + $500 USD):** Structured as an $850 sponsorship fee and a separate $500 attendee registration fee via `shop.hems-workshop.org` to align with internal corporate purchasing limits.
* **Sponsor Only / Non-Attending ($850 USD):** Remote workshop support with corporate poster display and website/program recognition.
